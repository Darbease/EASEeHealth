## Rationale for Coronary CT Angiography

Coronary CTA is the guideline-recommended next test for an intermediate-risk patient with stable chest pain and a non-diagnostic stress study. It provides direct, non-invasive assessment of coronary stenosis and plaque burden and will determine whether invasive catheter angiography is warranted or can be safely deferred, sparing the patient an unnecessary invasive procedure while resolving a clinically significant diagnostic question.
